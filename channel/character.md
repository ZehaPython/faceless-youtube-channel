# Character Bible: Atlas

**Status: look approved by the creator.** Master reference: `channel/reference/atlas-reference.webp`.

## Who he is

**Atlas** is a stocky, middle-aged **raccoon astronomer** who lives in a timber **cabin observatory high in the mountains**,
above a pine forest, a lake and a small sleeping town. He is our host and narrator. Every Short is one of his
night-time adventures into a corner of science.

- **Personality:** endlessly curious, warm, a little theatrical. He gets genuinely giddy about big ideas, then lowers
  his voice to share a secret. He is a teacher who still feels like a student.
- **Catchphrase (sparingly):** "Pack a sweater." He says it at the end of an episode, before the next adventure.
- **Why a raccoon:** nocturnal (it fits the starry night-time world), clever paws for tinkering, and naturally
  "masked" for a faceless channel. It also avoids the birds of the biggest channel in our niche.

## Look (reuse this text word for word in every prompt where he appears)

> Atlas, a stocky middle-aged raccoon astronomer with soft, detailed grey and brown fur, a dark eye mask,
> warm brown eyes and a kind, slightly smug smile, wearing thin round gold wire-rimmed glasses, a chunky
> mustard-yellow cable-knit cardigan with brown leather elbow patches and wooden buttons, a thick knitted
> pine-green scarf, and a worn brown leather satchel on a strap across his body. Big fluffy ringed tail.

- **Silhouette:** round and cozy, a big ringed tail, and glasses that catch the lantern light.
- **Expressions:** wide-eyed wonder (looking up at the stars), a content half-smile, a focused squint while writing.
- **Typical poses:** peering through his brass telescope, writing in a leather-bound star atlas with a quill,
  holding a steaming enamel mug, smiling at the camera.
- **Never:** clothing logos, modern phones, headphones, weapons, or anything that resembles Rocket Raccoon (no armour,
  no sci-fi gear).

## The Wonder Watch (series device)

An old **brass pocket watch** with a cracked glass face, inherited and unexplained. It doesn't just tell time:
at the end of each episode it **ticks, glows and points somewhere new**, and that is the next adventure.

- It links every Short into one continuous journey ("Episode 2 is where the watch pointed").
- It gives each outro a built-in cliffhanger, which encourages people to follow the channel.
- Its secrets can be revealed slowly over the series. The watch itself is a long-running mystery.
- He keeps it in his satchel or cardigan pocket. It hasn't appeared in the reference image yet, so introduce it
  with a clear close-up in Episode 1.

## Home base: the cabin observatory

A timber cabin observatory with large windows over snowy mountains, a pine forest, a lake and distant town lights.
Inside: a heavy wooden desk covered in parchment star charts, leather-bound books, a brass armillary globe, oil
lanterns, potted plants, and constellation sketches and moon drawings pinned to the walls. The sky outside is full
of stars and the Milky Way, sometimes with a shooting star, or a magenta-orange dusk over the peaks.
A sleepy grey cat called **Comet** (comic relief, never speaks) can appear in the background.
