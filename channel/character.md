# Character Bible: Atlas

**Status: proposal. Awaiting creator approval.**

## Who he is

**Atlas** is a stocky, middle-aged **raccoon astronomer** who lives in a cluttered attic observatory above a sleeping city.
He is our host and narrator. Every Short is one of his night-time adventures into a corner of science.

- **Personality:** endlessly curious, warm, a little theatrical. He gets genuinely giddy about big ideas, then lowers
  his voice to share a secret. He is a teacher who still feels like a student.
- **Catchphrase (sparingly):** "Pack a sweater." He says it at the end of an episode, before the next adventure.
- **Why a raccoon:** nocturnal (it fits the night-time lo-fi world), clever hands for tinkering, and naturally
  "masked" for a faceless channel. It also avoids the birds of the biggest channel in our niche.

## Look (reuse this text word for word in every image prompt)

> Atlas, a stocky middle-aged raccoon astronomer with soft grey-brown fur, a dark eye mask, warm amber eyes and a
> slightly greying muzzle, wearing round brass-rimmed spectacles, a chunky mustard-yellow knit cardigan with leather
> elbow patches, and a dark forest-green scarf. A battered brown leather field journal hangs from a strap across his chest.

- Silhouette: round and cozy, a big ringed tail, and spectacles that catch the lamp light.
- Expressions: wide-eyed wonder, a sly half-smile, focused squint over the journal.
- Never: human clothing logos, modern phones, headphones (that's the Lofi Girl's signature).

## The Wonder Watch (series device)

An old **brass pocket watch** with a cracked glass face, inherited and unexplained. It doesn't just tell time:
at the end of each episode it **ticks, glows and points somewhere new**, and that is the next adventure.

- It links every Short into one continuous journey ("Episode 2 is where the watch pointed").
- It gives each outro a built-in cliffhanger, which encourages people to follow the channel.
- Its secrets can be revealed slowly over the series. The watch itself is a long-running mystery.

## Home base

The attic observatory: a wooden desk under a warm amber lamp, a brass telescope at the round window, stacks of books,
star charts pinned to the beams, potted plants, a steaming mug, and a sleepy grey cat called **Comet** (comic relief,
never speaks). Each episode starts or ends here.
