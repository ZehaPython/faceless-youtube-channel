## 1. Create the channel (10 min)

Use a **brand channel** (not your personal name). It can have its own name, and you can add co-managers later.

1. Go to **youtube.com** and sign in with your Google account.
2. Click your profile picture (top right) → **Settings** → **Add or manage your channel(s)** → **Create a channel**.
3. Name: **Atlas and the Universe** → Create.
4. Handle: when asked (or later in Studio → Customization → Basic info), set **@AtlasAndTheUniverse**.
   (It was free on 2026-10-25. YouTube confirms availability when you claim it.)

## 2. Branding (5 min)

**YouTube Studio** (studio.youtube.com) → make sure the new channel is selected (top right) → **Customization**:

- **Profile → Picture:** upload `avatar-800.png`.
- **Profile → Banner image:** upload `banner-2560x1440.png`. Check the preview for desktop, mobile and TV.
- **Profile → Description:** paste the text below.
- **Profile → Links (optional):** add later (e.g. Instagram/TikTok if you cross-post).
- Click **Publish** (top right).

### Channel description (paste this)

> Join Atlas, a curious raccoon astronomer, on short adventures to the strangest corners of the universe: from rocks
> that make oxygen at the bottom of the ocean to crystals that tick in time, exploding stars and black holes.
>
> Every episode is under two minutes, packed with real, sourced science, and told like a story.
> New adventures every week. 🦝🔭✨
>
> Sources for every fact are in each video's description.

**Keywords** (Settings → Channel → Basic info → Keywords):
science, space, astronomy, ocean, deep sea, physics, universe, science shorts, explained, Atlas

## 3. Channel settings (3 min)

Studio → **Settings**:
- **Channel → Basic info → Country:** your country.
- **Channel → Advanced settings → Audience:** "No, set this channel as not made for kids".
- **Upload defaults → Basic info → Category:** Science & Technology.
- **Upload defaults → Advanced settings → Language:** English.

## 4. Upload the episodes (5 min each)

Studio → **Create** (top right) → **Upload videos** → choose the MP4.
Vertical videos under 3 minutes become **Shorts** automatically.

**Details page** (copy from the episode's `upload.md`):
1. **Title** and **Description** (with the sources and the music credit if required).
2. **Audience:** No, it's not made for kids.
3. **Show more → Altered content:** answer **No** (stylized animation, nothing realistic). Answering Yes is always allowed if unsure.
4. **Tags:** optional (use the hashtags in the description instead).
5. **Category:** Science & Technology.

**Video elements:** skip (not available for Shorts).
**Checks:** wait for the copyright check. The music is from the YouTube Audio Library, so it should pass.
**Visibility:** **Schedule** (see the plan below) or **Public**.

## 5. Suggested publishing plan

| When | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1, ~17:00 your time | **Episode 2: "These Rocks Are Breathing?"** | Strongest episode (new format, music): the channel's first impression |
| Day 3, ~17:00 | **Episode 1: "This Crystal Has a Heartbeat?"** | Older format, but solid. Its outro ("pointing down... the bottom of the ocean") leads nicely to Episode 2 |
| Then | 2–3 new Shorts per week | Consistency matters more than volume for a new channel |

Afternoon and evening posting is a common starting point. Check **Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on
YouTube"** after a few weeks and adjust.

## 6. After publishing

- In the first 48 hours, check **Analytics → Content → Shorts**: "Viewed vs. swiped away" (aim > 70%) and
  average percentage viewed (aim > 80%).
- Reply to the first comments, which helps early reach.
- Record results in `published/<slug>.md` so future scripts can learn from them.
