# Visual Style Guide

Every frame of every video follows this guide. The content agent reads it before making anything.

## Format

- **YouTube Shorts only:** vertical **9:16**, **1080 × 1920 px**, 30 fps, under 60 seconds.
- **Safe zones:** YouTube's interface covers parts of the screen, so keep text and key subjects inside the safe area:
  - Top 250 px: avoid (search bar, channel name)
  - Bottom 420 px: avoid (title, description, music tag)
  - Right 140 px: avoid (like, comment and share buttons)
  - **Safe area:** x 60–940, y 250–1500

## Look: flat 2D animation

- Clean **flat vector** illustration: simple geometric shapes, no outlines or only thin ones, and no photorealism.
- **Soft depth:** layered shapes, subtle gradients in the background only, and simple long shadows.
- **Cute but smart:** rounded characters and creatures with simple dot eyes. Objects get personality.
- **Dark backgrounds** (deep navy or purple) with **bright, saturated** subjects that glow against them.
- Each frame has **one clear focal point**. If you need a second look to find the subject, simplify.

## Colour palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background (deep) | Midnight navy | `#141B3C` |
| Background (mid) | Indigo | `#2A2F6B` |
| Primary accent | Warm orange | `#FF8A3D` |
| Secondary accent | Teal | `#2EC4B6` |
| Highlight | Sun yellow | `#FFD23F` |
| Danger / alert | Coral red | `#FF4F5E` |
| Soft light | Lavender | `#B8B5FF` |
| Text | Off-white | `#F7F4EA` |

Use at most 3 accent colours in a single frame.

## Typography (on-screen text)

- **Font:** a bold rounded sans-serif, e.g. *Nunito Black*, *Poppins ExtraBold* or *Baloo 2*. CapCut and Canva both have these.
- **Hook text:** 90–120 px, off-white, max **6 words**, 1–2 lines, centred within the safe area.
- Highlight one key word in **sun yellow** or **orange**.
- Captions during narration: 60–70 px with a subtle dark shadow, placed at y ≈ 1250–1450.

## The first frame (the hook frame)

The first frame decides whether people swipe past. It must:
1. Show the topic's **main subject** big and readable within half a second.
2. Carry the **hook text** (max 6 words) that creates curiosity or stakes.
3. Contain **something surprising**: an odd scale, an impossible situation, or a character in trouble.
4. Look good **as a still**, because it also works as the Shorts cover image.

## AI generation prompt base (Higgsfield)

Start every image prompt with this base, then describe the scene:

> Flat 2D vector illustration, Kurzgesagt-inspired minimal style, clean geometric shapes, soft gradients, dark navy background (#141B3C), bright saturated accents of orange (#FF8A3D), teal (#2EC4B6) and yellow (#FFD23F), cute rounded characters with simple dot eyes, soft glow, no outlines, no text, no photorealism, vertical 9:16 composition, subject centred in the upper-middle area,

Add on-screen text later in the editor, not in the AI image, because AI-generated text is unreliable.

**Originality:** the style is *inspired by* flat science animation, but never copy Kurzgesagt's characters (such as their birds), logos or specific scenes.
