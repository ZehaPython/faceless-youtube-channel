## Accuracy check (against the research brief)

- "Making oxygen in total darkness" is hedged in the body ("scientists measured", "maybe", "study is wrong"). The hook states the claim as the mystery, and the twist shows it's disputed.
- "That should be impossible": oxygen production on the seafloor without light was thought impossible (Northwestern coverage, fact 1).
- "Four kilometers down" matches fact 2. "This glass won't hold" is Atlas's story danger.
- "In 2024, scientists measured oxygen rising" matches fact 1.
- "Grew for millions of years" matches fact 3. "About a volt... most of an AA battery": up to ~0.95 V vs 1.5 V (fact 4).
- "*Maybe* they split seawater" is hedged (fact 4).
- "Mining companies want these rocks for car batteries" matches fact 6. "Other scientists say it's wrong" matches fact 7.
- "Mining them could take oxygen away from deep-sea life" is conditional ("if it's real"). Geiger said mining should avoid depleting "the oxygen source for deep-sea life" (Northwestern, fact 1 source).
- "Robots are diving down to settle it" matches fact 8. Recheck before publishing.
- "Every oxygen atom in your lungs was forged inside a star" matches fact 9.
