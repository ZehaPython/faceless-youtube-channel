## Accuracy check (against the research brief)

- "Four kilometers down" matches fact 2. "Air running low" is Atlas's story danger, not a science claim.
- "In 2024, scientists measured oxygen rising" matches fact 1.
- "Grew for millions of years" matches fact 3. "Almost a volt" matches fact 4. "*Maybe* they split seawater" keeps it hedged.
- "Mining companies want these rocks for car batteries" matches fact 6. "Other scientists say the study is wrong" matches fact 7.
- "Robots are diving down to settle it" refers to the announced expeditions (fact 8). Recheck before publishing.
- "If it's real... oxygen doesn't need the Sun at all" is about this seafloor source. It's a conditional, not a claim that it's proven.
- "Every oxygen atom in your lungs was forged inside a star" matches fact 9.
