## Accuracy check (against the research brief)

- "These rocks are making oxygen" is the hook's mystery; the body hedges it ("scientists measured", "maybe", "the study is wrong").
- **"Sunlit plankton make about half the oxygen you breathe"**: roughly half of Earth's oxygen production is from the ocean (fact 10).
- "No sun. Nothing should make it": no photosynthesis at 4,000 m (facts 1–2). Deep water holds oxygen carried down from the surface; the line is about *making* it.
- "In 2024, scientists measured oxygen rising" matches fact 1.
- **"Grow just millimeters every million years"**: 1–20 mm per million years (fact 12). The fact pop says "~1 cm", which sits inside the range.
- "About a volt... most of an AA battery": up to ~0.95 V vs 1.5 V (fact 4). "*Maybe* they split seawater" is hedged.
- "Mining companies want these rocks for car batteries" matches fact 6. "Other scientists say the study is wrong" matches fact 7.
- **"Over five thousand species, nine in ten unknown to science"**: 5,578 recorded in the CCZ, 88–92% new (fact 11).
- **"Almost two-thirds oxygen, by weight"**: 61–65% (fact 13). "Every atom forged inside a star" matches fact 9.
- Atlas's air running out is story, not a science claim.
