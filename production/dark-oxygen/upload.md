## Title (recommended)
These Rocks Are Breathing?

Alternatives: "Oxygen Where There's No Sun?" · "The Ocean Floor Makes Oxygen. Or Does It?" · "Why Mining Companies Want These Rocks"

## Description

4,000 meters down, with no sunlight, scientists measured oxygen being made on the seafloor. The suspects: polymetallic
nodules, metal lumps that grow just millimeters every million years. Here's why miners want them, and what's at stake.

⚠️ Good to know: the 2024 "dark oxygen" study is still debated. Other scientists and a mining company have challenged
the measurements, and new expeditions are underway to test it. We'll update you when the results are in.
