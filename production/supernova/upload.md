## Title (recommended)
How Long Does a Star Live?

Alternatives: "Will Our Sun Explode?" · "What Happens When a Star Dies?"

## Description

Our Sun will shine for about 10 billion years. A giant star? Only a few million. Atlas is standing next to one
at the very end of its life: what a star is made of, why big stars die young, what happens when they run out of
fuel, and what's left behind.

Good to know: scientists still debate where all the gold comes from. Supernovae make some, but colliding neutron
stars probably make much more. Our Sun is too small to explode: in about 5 billion years it will swell into a red
giant and end as a white dwarf.
