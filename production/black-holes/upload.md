## Description

A black hole isn't a hole at all: it's a huge amount of matter crushed into a tiny space. Squeeze all of Earth
that hard and it would be about the size of a marble. Where black holes come from, why they're black, what the
event horizon really is, and the giant waiting at the heart of our own galaxy: Sagittarius A*.

Good to know: being stretched "like spaghetti" before reaching the edge happens near small, star-sized black holes.
At a giant one like Sagittarius A*, you could cross the edge without feeling much at first.
