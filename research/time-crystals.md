## One-line idea

A time crystal is matter whose pattern repeats **in time** instead of in space. When it is pushed with a regular beat,
it settles into **its own** steady rhythm, and it doesn't heat up and fall apart the way normal matter would.

## Careful wording (avoid misleading claims)

- ❌ "It moves forever with no energy." ✅ "It keeps its own rhythm without heating up or wearing out."
- ❌ "Perpetual motion machine." ✅ "It *looks* like perpetual motion, but you can't get any energy out of it."
- ❌ "Time travel." Time crystals don't involve time travel. Fine as a *joke* in the outro, never as a claim.
- ✅ "A new phase of matter" is acceptable (used by Physics World's headline about the Google result).

## Story angles

1. **"Physics said it was impossible."** The proposal, the impossibility proof, then the loophole. Best hook.
2. **A heart that ignores the drummer:** atoms kicked on every beat flip on every *second* beat.
3. **New phase of matter:** solid, liquid, gas... and *time-crystalline*.
